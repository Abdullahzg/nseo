# Video T1

Feature/landing-page template for a video-generation tool or use case. Section order matters — follow it
exactly. *(Word counts below were bumped +15 per section from the original spec on 2026-07-29 — treat
these as the current targets, not the smaller numbers if you see them referenced anywhere older.)*

1. **H1** — heading + 65-word subtext
2. **H2** — heading + 75 words content
3. **H2** — heading + 75 words content
4. **H2** — heading + 75 words content
5. **H2** — "how it works" style heading + 3 H3s, 55 words each. Pattern: step 1 (e.g. enter prompt),
   step 2 (e.g. generate), step 3 (e.g. hit download) — adapt the 3 steps to the actual product flow, but
   keep it to exactly 3 steps.
6. **H2** — heading + 55 words content
7. **H2** — heading + 55 words content
8. **H2** — heading + 55 words content
9. **Interlinking block** — 5 to 10 relevant internal pages
10. **Testimonials** — 5, 45 words each
11. **FAQs** — 6, with answers, 65 words each

Total H2 content sections: 6 (three at 75 words, three at 55 words) plus the 3-step "how it works" H2.

## Notes for filling this in

- H1 should carry the primary keyword; subtext (65 words) is the hook for the page — a couple of sentences,
  not a single fragment.
- The first three 75-word H2s are typically benefit/value-prop sections — lead with what the tool does for
  the user, not a feature list yet.
- The 55-word H2s (after "how it works") are typically supporting features, use cases, or differentiators
  — shorter and punchier than the opening three, but with enough room now to add one supporting detail or
  example per section.
- The interlinking block goes near the end, right before testimonials/FAQs, not up top (contrast with
  Image T7, which places it right after the H1).
- Testimonials and FAQs are the last two blocks, in that order, with no CTA block explicitly called out in
  this template — a closing CTA can live inside the last 55-word H2 or the FAQ section unless told
  otherwise.
