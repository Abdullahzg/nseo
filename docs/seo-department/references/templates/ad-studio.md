# Ad Studio

Feature/landing-page template for Ad Studio (or a similarly product-flow-driven page). No CTA block and
no interlinking block explicitly specified here, unlike other templates — don't add them unless asked.
*(Word counts below were bumped +15 per section from the original spec on 2026-07-29 — treat these as the
current targets.)*

1. **H1** — heading + 35-word subtext
2. **H2** — heading + 4 H3s, each with its own heading + 45 words content (a 4-item feature/benefit grid)
3. **H2** — heading + 3 use-case bullets, each with a 30-word subtext
4. **H2** — "how it works" style heading (source example: *"How to Turn a Product URL into a Video?"* —
   adapt the question to the actual product flow) + 3 H3s, 35 words each. Pattern: step 1 (e.g. enter
   prompt), step 2 (e.g. generate), step 3 (e.g. hit download) — keep it to exactly 3 steps.
5. **H2** — heading + 6 H3s, 45 words each (a larger, 6-item feature/benefit grid)
6. **Testimonials** — heading + 6 testimonials (word count per testimonial wasn't specified in the source
   spec — ask, or default to ~45 words each to match the other templates' bumped counts, and flag that you
   defaulted)
7. **FAQs** — 6, with answers, 65 words each

## Notes for filling this in

- Unlike MCP, this template has no eyebrow text called out — just plain headings throughout.
- Two different "grid" sections (item 2 at 4×45 words, item 5 at 6×45 words) — don't collapse them into
  one or confuse which comes before vs. after the "how it works" section; the smaller grid comes first.
- The use-case bullets (item 3) are short — 30 words each, closer to a tight one-liner than a full
  paragraph.
- No CTA block and no interlinking block in this template, unlike Video T1 / Image T7 / MCP — leave them
  out unless told otherwise.
