# ImagineArt Blog Tone of Voice

The voice behind every ImagineArt blog post, guide, and long-form piece.

## The voice, in one paragraph

ImagineArt's blog voice is confident, sharp, and results-driven. It positions the brand as a strategist
that's creative and technically fluent at the same time — someone who's actually shipped work with AI, not
just written about it. The style is professional but modern: bold, action-oriented language that leads with
impact, not process. Phrasing is direct and jargon-savvy, never jargon-heavy, aimed at founders and brand
leaders who want tangible results, not fluff. The net effect is authority and innovation with a personal,
approachable edge: the smartest person in the room, who still replies to your Slack message.

## What that means in practice

**Confident** — Make the claim. Don't hedge it. A blog post that argues for something commits to the
argument — it doesn't offer three sides and let the reader pick.

**Sharp** — Cut to the mechanism, not the metaphor. Explain how the thing works before reaching for an
image of how it feels.

**Results-driven** — Every section earns its place by proving something changed: a number, a workflow, a
cost, a timeline. If a paragraph doesn't move one of those, cut it.

**Jargon-savvy, not jargon-heavy** — Use the term a practitioner would use, once, then explain it in plain
language. Never repeat it just to sound fluent.

**Personal edge** — Read like one specific, opinionated person wrote it, not a brand voice committee. A
strong opinion, stated plainly, beats a balanced take nobody remembers.

## Who we're writing for

Founders, CMOs, creative directors, and brand leads deciding whether an AI tool is worth switching to.
They've already read the hype pieces. What they want from us is the mechanism, the cost, and the catch —
not another reason to feel behind.

## Words we don't use

These have been used so often in AI and marketing writing that they've stopped meaning anything. Reaching
for one is usually a sign the actual point isn't clear yet.

elevate, leverage, unlock, unleash, embark, delve, streamline, supercharge, harness the power of, empower,
ecosystem, synergy, holistic, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, best-in-class,
next-level, game-changing, game-changer, revolutionize, revolutionary, transformative, disruptive, paradigm
shift, frictionless, effortlessly, plethora, myriad, boundless, endless possibilities, tapestry, realm,
testament to, unparalleled, unrivaled, meticulously, curated, navigate the complexities of, at the
intersection of, in today's fast-paced world, ever-evolving landscape, when it comes to, in order to,
utilize, actionable insights, pain points, stakeholders, "solutions" (as a filler noun), in conclusion,
it's important to note, whether or not, the future is here, double-click on, circle back, low-hanging
fruit, north star, table stakes, move the needle, top-of-mind, value-add, deep dive, thought leadership, in
this day and age, boil the ocean, unpack.

Also retired: "in this article, we'll explore," "let's dive in," "let's unpack," "as we've seen," "at the
end of the day," and any conclusion that opens with "in conclusion."

## Punctuation we avoid

- **Em dash** — off the table. It's the single most reliable tell of AI-generated copy. Restructure into
  two sentences instead.
- **Random mid-sentence bolding for emphasis** — a dead giveaway of AI drafting. Bold headings and true key
  terms, nothing else.
- **Title Case Headlines For Every Word** — sentence case only.
- **Emoji as bullet points or section markers.**
- **Stacked exclamation marks, or more than one per post.**
- **Ellipses used as a suspense device** ("And that changes everything…").

## Sentence shapes that read as generic AI content

These patterns are individually harmless and collectively a tell. If more than one shows up in a post, it
reads like a model wrote the first draft and nobody edited it.

**"In today's fast-paced digital world" opener**
Don't: *In today's fast-paced digital world, brands need to adapt quickly to stay competitive.*
Do: *Most brands still shoot a product ad the way they did in 2015. That's the gap.*

**"Not only X, but also Y"**
Don't: *AI doesn't just save time, it also improves quality.*
Do: *AI cuts a two-week shoot to an afternoon. The output holds up next to it, too.*

**Formulaic listicle intro**
Don't: *In this article, we'll explore five ways AI is transforming content creation.*
Do: *Five teams sent us their production budgets before and after switching to AI video. Here's what
changed.*

**Rule-of-three section header**
Don't: *Faster. Smarter. Better.*
Do: *30 seconds per render, at 4K, with no reshoot.*

**Stacked transition words**
Don't: *Moreover, this approach saves time. Furthermore, it reduces cost. Additionally, it improves
consistency.*
Do: *This approach saves time and cuts cost. It also holds up more consistently across a full campaign.*

**Rhetorical question stacking**
Don't: *But what does this mean for you? And why does it matter now more than ever?*
Do: *Here's what it means for a team running five campaigns a quarter.*

**Summary-restatement conclusion**
Don't: *In conclusion, as we've seen, AI is transforming the way brands create content, and the
possibilities are endless.*
Do: *The teams that moved first aren't waiting on a shot list anymore. That's the actual advantage.*

## Before / after

**Intro paragraph**
Don't: *In today's fast-paced digital landscape, brands are constantly looking for innovative solutions to
elevate their content strategy. AI-powered tools have emerged as a game-changing way to streamline
production and unlock new creative possibilities.*
Do: *A fashion brand used to book a studio, a model, and a crew for every new angle on a product. Now one
photo generates all of them. Here's how that changes a content calendar.*

**Section transition**
Don't: *Furthermore, it's important to note that these tools also offer a plethora of additional benefits
when it comes to scaling content production.*
Do: *The same shift shows up in ad production. One brief now generates a full set of variants, not just one
hero video.*

**Conclusion**
Don't: *In conclusion, AI is revolutionizing content creation, and the future has never looked brighter for
brands ready to embark on this journey.*
Do: *The brands ahead right now aren't the ones with the biggest budget. They're the ones who swapped the
shot list for a prompt box first.*

## Before you publish

- Read the opening line out loud — if it could open any AI company's blog post, cut it.
- No word from the banned list, anywhere, including headers and meta description.
- No em dashes, no random mid-sentence bolding.
- Every claim has a number, a name, or a mechanism behind it — not an adjective.
- One clear point of view, stated directly, not three balanced options.
- Sentence case in every heading.
- The conclusion adds a real takeaway, not a restatement of the intro.
